## Days
### Monday
- recipe: overnight-oats
- prep:
  - Mix oats, milk, yogurt, and chia seeds in a jar.
  - Chop berries and refrigerate everything overnight.
- cook:
  - No cooking needed; stir before eating.
- split:
  - Keep one jar for breakfast and one for Wednesday.
- eat:
  - Eat one jar cold in the morning.

### Tuesday
- recipe: sheet-pan-chicken-vegetables
- prep:
  - Dice vegetables and toss with olive oil, garlic, and paprika.
  - Pat chicken dry and season well.
- cook:
  - Roast chicken and vegetables at 425°F for 25-30 minutes.
- split:
  - Save half for Thursday lunch.
- eat:
  - Eat one hot serving for dinner.

### Wednesday
- recipe: turkey-chili
- prep:
  - Dice onion and bell pepper, rinse beans.
  - Measure spices and tomato base.
- cook:
  - Brown turkey, sauté vegetables, then simmer all ingredients for 30 minutes.
- split:
  - Portion into three containers.
- eat:
  - Eat one bowl tonight, save two for Friday and freezer.

### Thursday
- recipe: salmon-rice-bowls
- prep:
  - Cook rice and prep cucumber-carrot topping.
  - Mix soy-ginger sauce.
- cook:
  - Bake salmon at 400°F for 12-15 minutes.
- split:
  - Build two bowls; refrigerate one for Saturday.
- eat:
  - Eat one bowl warm for dinner.

### Friday
- recipe: turkey-chili
- prep:
  - Reheat saved chili with a splash of water.
- cook:
  - Simmer for 5-8 minutes until hot.
- split:
  - Keep freezer portion for next week.
- eat:
  - Eat with chopped cilantro and lime.

### Saturday
- recipe: salmon-rice-bowls
- prep:
  - Reheat rice and salmon separately.
- cook:
  - Warm in microwave 2-3 minutes.
- split:
  - Keep any extra vegetables for Sunday lunch.
- eat:
  - Eat cold or warm as preferred.

### Sunday

- prep:

- cook:

- split:
