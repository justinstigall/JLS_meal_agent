# Turkey Chili
- source: https://www.simplyrecipes.com/recipes/turkey_chili/

## Ingredients
- 1 lb | ground turkey
- 1 cup | onion
- 1 cup | bell pepper
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 can | kidney beans
- 1 can | black beans
- 1 can | crushed tomatoes
- 1 tbsp | chili powder
- 1 tsp | cumin
- 1/2 tsp | salt

## Steps
1. Brown ground turkey in a pot over medium heat.
2. Add onion, bell pepper, and garlic; cook until softened.
3. Stir in beans, tomatoes, chili powder, cumin, and salt.
4. Simmer uncovered for 30 minutes, stirring occasionally.
