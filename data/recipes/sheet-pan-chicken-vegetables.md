# Sheet Pan Chicken and Vegetables
- source: https://www.eatingwell.com/recipe/252487/sheet-pan-chicken-thighs-with-brussels-sprouts-gnocchi/

## Ingredients
- 1 lb | chicken thighs
- 2 cups | broccoli florets
- 1 cup | carrots
- 1 cup | bell pepper
- 2 tbsp | olive oil
- 2 cloves | garlic
- 1 tsp | paprika
- 1/2 tsp | salt

## Steps
1. Heat oven to 425°F.
2. Toss vegetables with olive oil, garlic, paprika, and salt.
3. Arrange seasoned chicken and vegetables on a sheet pan.
4. Roast for 25-30 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.
