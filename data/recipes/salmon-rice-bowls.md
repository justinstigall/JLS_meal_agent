# Salmon Rice Bowls
- source: https://www.bonappetit.com/recipe/salmon-rice-bowl

## Ingredients
- 2 fillets | salmon
- 1 cup | jasmine rice
- 1 cup | cucumber
- 1 cup | carrot
- 2 tbsp | soy sauce
- 1 tbsp | rice vinegar
- 1 tsp | grated ginger
- 1 tsp | sesame oil

## Steps
1. Cook rice according to package directions.
2. Whisk soy sauce, vinegar, ginger, and sesame oil.
3. Bake salmon at 400°F for 12-15 minutes.
4. Assemble bowls with rice, salmon, cucumber, carrot, and sauce.
