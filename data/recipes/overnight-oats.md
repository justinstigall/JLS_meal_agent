# Overnight Oats
- source: https://www.bbcgoodfood.com/recipes/overnight-oats

## Ingredients
- 1 cup | rolled oats
- 1 cup | milk
- 1/2 cup | Greek yogurt
- 1 tbsp | chia seeds
- 1 cup | mixed berries
- 1 tsp | honey

## Steps
1. Stir oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, and honey in a jar.
2. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
3. Top with berries before serving.
